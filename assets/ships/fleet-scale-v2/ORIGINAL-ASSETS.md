# Original asset declaration

All mesh geometry in AUR Fleet Scale v2 was authored procedurally from scratch for this repository. No downloaded, scanned, marketplace, Sketchfab, or other third-party model geometry is included.

Generated raster concept sheets and model-locked material paint-overs are included as design and QA references. They are not substituted geometry. The Blender source remains fully editable and the GLB/FBX files are exports of that original source.
